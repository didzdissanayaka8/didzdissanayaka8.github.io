About

M.Eng in Computer Engineering, Memorial University of Newfoundland.

I'm a Research Engineer at Memorial University of Newfoundland working on explainable AI, deep reinforcement learning, and SLAM for autonomous UAV navigation. My work sits at the intersection of perception (lidar, camera, IMU, GPS sensor fusion), learning-based control, and making those systems interpretable enough to trust in the field.

I like problems that don't stay in simulation — testing localization on a Bell 412 helicopter, applying Integrated Gradients to explain a drone's obstacle-avoidance and path planning decisions in real time, or getting a battery management system into a real electric supercar.

DWG-04

Tick Stick — AI-Powered Tick Detection Device

Led software development for a real-time image classification and tick detection system, reaching over 92% accuracy with a MobileNet neural network. Integrated with an ESP32-S3 Eye board to produce the first working prototype, in close collaboration with investors and a cross-functional team.

PythonTensorFlowC++ESP32-S3
DWG-05

Self-Balancing Track Robot on Uneven Terrain via Arm Maneuvering

Developed an algorithm for a continuous-track wheeled robot to self-balance and navigate uneven terrain using arm maneuvers, applying Zero Moment Point (ZMP) principles and the support polygon of the track and ground. Aimed at disaster management and field robotics.

C++ROSGazebo

End-to-End 3D Reconstruction of Ancient Artifacts from Images

An autonomous end-to-end system to reconstruct artifacts in 3D from 2D images: a rotating mechanical platform captures photographs across a hemispherical area, an algorithm outputs a high-quality 3D reconstruction, and a mobile app retrieves and lets users interact with the resulting models.
